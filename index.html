ACQUISITION PROTOCOL & SECURITY

How secure is the acquisition process?

All transactions are processed exclusively through our Official Authorized Marketplaces. These platforms act as a secure bridge, ensuring your payment is handled within a regulated and 100% safe environment with professional Buyer Protection.

Is my financial data protected?

Yes. We utilize industry-leading payment processors provided by our partner marketplaces. Your financial data is fully encrypted and protected by global security standards.

How is the source code delivered?

To ensure absolute efficiency, the delivery system is fully automated. Once the authorized marketplace verifies your payment, you will receive an immediate download link to the complete repository.

Do you provide technical support?

While the marketplace handles the secure transaction, I provide direct technical support for any structural issues. You can reach the operator through the details in your purchase receipt.
